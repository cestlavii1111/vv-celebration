What to Wear

Earthy Chic

Day

From arrival through the pool party and games, keep it easy and breathable — light fabrics, sandals or shoes you don't mind on grass, and a swimsuit under your outfit if you'd like to take a dip in the pool.

Evening

As we move into dinner, feel free to elevate your look a touch. Vanessa and Valeria will be wearing their dresses to take photos with everyone. Temperatures start to drop once the sun goes down, so a light jacket or wrap is a good idea.
